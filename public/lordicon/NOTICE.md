# Icons by Lordicon

These free assets are used in Damon's Archive under the Lordicon Free License.
Attribution: https://lordicon.com/
License: https://lordicon.com/docs/license/free

The app serves the SVG and Lottie JSON locally. Only icons used by this app are included.
UI colors and orientation are applied at render time. Wired Outline strokes are increased from 12 to 24 source pixels in the SVG and Lottie data to remain readable at small UI sizes.
